Perform

Lyrics that follow the music.

A full-screen stage view with big, readable lyrics and chords. Attach the recording and the current line lights up as the song plays — or let the sheet scroll on its own, paced to the song.

  • Synced lyrics — Auto Sync finds timed lyrics with Shazam, or tap along by hand.
  • Hands-free auto-scroll, paced by the song's duration or its BPM.
  • The set rolls on — each song advances when its audio ends; jump anywhere with the song picker.
  • Fix a chord mid-show without leaving the stage view.
Setlists

Build the set. Play it in order.

Drag songs into order, split the night into sets and see exactly how long each one runs. Hit Play Set and go — lock it before showtime and nothing changes by accident.

  • Sections like Set 1, Break and Encore, each with its own song count and length.
  • A gig clock shows the section, time left, and whether you run over or ahead.
  • Locked setlists keep every song's stem mix, exactly as you set it up.
  • Save As… turns this set into the start of the next one.
Stem separation  7 days free

Remove vocals. Mute any part. Play along.

Split a song into 2, 4 or 6 stems — vocals, drums, bass, guitar, piano and the rest — right on your iPhone or iPad. Mute what you play, and the band plays on without you.

  • On device — the AI model runs on your iPhone or iPad; your audio is never uploaded.
  • Every stem on its own waveform; tap an instrument to mute it.
  • Practice tools: slow down without changing pitch, shift the key, loop a passage A–B.
  • Your mix is remembered per song and per setlist.

Built for practice, not studio mixing: it helps you hear each part more clearly. Separation quality depends on the song. Free for 7 days from install, then part of Pro.

Scores & chords

Sheet music and chord charts, on stage.

Attach scores, parts or lyric sheets and swipe through them full screen. Songs with chords get a chart for every chord, for guitar or piano.

  • Song images — scores, instrument parts, chord charts or lyric sheets, in your order.
  • Chord charts with guitar voicings and piano inversions, rendered offline.
  • Transpose live Pro — the chords change, the lyrics never do.
  • Import PDF scores Pro as swipeable pages.
Library

Every song you play, found in a second.

Search, filter by artist and label, sort A–Z or by what changed last. Bring songs in from the Online Song Catalog, Apple Notes, text and PDF files, or audio files that fill in their own details.

  • Multi-filter by artists and labels at once.
  • Paste as New Song — copied lyrics become a song in one tap.
  • Undo a delete for 7 seconds, back into every setlist.
  • Offline-first, with private iCloud sync across your devices.
