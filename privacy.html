Your music stays yours.

Gig Songbook does not collect, store, transmit, sell, or share your personal data.

No accountYou never create a profile with us.
No trackingNo analytics, ads, or tracking SDKs.
No developer cloudYour library never lives on our servers.

Data collection

All data you enter — songs, setlists, audio, stems, score images, and settings — is stored on your device and, if you enable iCloud, in your private iCloud container. It is not accessible to the app developer or any third party.

iCloud sync

If iCloud is enabled, your library syncs across your devices through Apple’s iCloud service using private CloudKit document storage. Apple’s iCloud Terms of Service and Privacy Policy apply.

You can disable access in your device’s Settings under Apple Account → iCloud → Gig Songbook.

Third-party services

Gig Songbook does not use analytics, advertising SDKs, third-party crash reporting, or behavioral tracking. The following network requests happen only when you ask for the related feature:

Lyrics lookupSends the song title and artist to api.lyrics.ovh or lrclib.net.
Online Song CatalogSends the search query to lrclib.net.
Auto Sync via ShazamSends a short audio sample to Apple Shazam, then requests timed lyrics.
Chord diagramsRequests the chord symbol from a public GitHub repository.

These requests do not include your name, email, location, device ID, or other personal information.

Stem separation

Stem separation runs on your iPhone or iPad. Your audio is not uploaded to a server for processing.

In-app purchases

The optional one-time Gig Songbook Pro purchase is processed entirely by Apple through StoreKit. Gig Songbook does not receive or store your payment details.

Children

The app is rated 4+ and does not knowingly collect data from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, this page will show the revised date and the updated terms.
